# PALMORIA-GROUP HR ANALYTICS
HR analytics case study investigating gender representation, pay equity, salary compliance, performance ratings, and employee compensation using Power BI, DAX and Excel.

## Project Overview
Palmoria Group is a manufacturing company operating across different regions and departments in Nigeria. This project analyzes the company's employee data to assess workforce composition, gender-related pay differences, salary compliance, employee performance, and performance-based compensation.

Using Excel for data preparation and Power BI with DAX for analysis and visualization, the project transforms raw employee data into an interactive HR analytics dashboard. The analysis provides insights that can support more informed decisions around workforce management, compensation, gender equity, and employee performance.

## Business Problem

Palmoria Group faces important HR and compensation challenges that require data-driven investigation. Concerns around gender representation, differences in employee pay, salary compliance, performance evaluation, and bonus allocation can affect employee satisfaction, fairness, and overall organizational performance.

The company needs a clearer understanding of its workforce and compensation structure across regions and departments. This analysis therefore investigates employee demographics, gender representation, salary differences, performance ratings, compliance with the ₦90,000 minimum salary threshold, and performance-based compensation to identify patterns, gaps, and areas requiring management attention.

## Project Objectives

The analysis was designed to:
- Examine overall workforce composition and gender representation.
- Analyze gender distribution across regions and departments.
- Assess performance ratings across genders.
- Compare average salaries by gender, region, and department.
- Identify gender-related pay differences across departments.
- Evaluate employee salary compliance against the ₦90,000 minimum salary threshold.
- Analyze bonus eligibility, performance ratings, and bonus payouts.
- Examine overall employee compensation and identify areas requiring management attention.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to support fair compensation and effective workforce management.

## Data Preparation & Cleaning

The employee dataset was reviewed and prepared before analysis to improve data quality and ensure reliable results. The preparation process included:

- Reviewing employee records for missing and inconsistent values.
- Cleaning and standardizing relevant categorical fields such as gender, department, region, and performance ratings.
- Reviewing salary and other numerical fields for consistency.
- Identifying employees with missing or undisclosed gender information.
- Preparing salary and employee attributes for analysis across departments and regions.
- Creating calculated fields and measures required for salary compliance, bonus eligibility, compensation, and other HR metrics.
- Validating the prepared data before developing the Power BI dashboard.

## Analysis Performed

### Workforce & Gender Analysis

- Analyzed overall employee gender distribution.
- Examined gender representation across regions and departments.
- Assessed performance ratings by gender.
- Identified areas where gender representation was more or less balanced.

### Salary & Pay Equity Analysis

- Compared average salaries by gender and region.
- Analyzed salary differences between male and female employees.
- Investigated gender pay gaps across departments.
- Examined employee salary distribution using salary bands.

### Salary Compliance Analysis

- Evaluated employee salaries against the ₦90,000 minimum salary threshold.
- Calculated the number and percentage of employees meeting or falling below the threshold.
- Identified the extent of salary compliance across the workforce.

### Performance & Compensation Analysis

- Examined employee performance ratings.
- Analyzed bonus eligibility based on employee performance.
- Identified employees who were not rated.
- Calculated bonus payouts and overall employee compensation.

## Key Findings

The analysis revealed several important workforce and compensation insights:

- **Gender representation:** The workforce showed relatively balanced male and female representation overall, although the distribution varied across specific regions and departments.
- **Gender pay differences:** Measurable salary differences were observed between male and female employees, with the largest gender pay gaps concentrated in specific departments.
- **Salary compliance:** Only **292 employees (30.87%)** met the ₦90,000 minimum salary threshold, while **654 employees (69.13%)** were below the threshold.
- **Bonus eligibility:** **875 employees** were identified as bonus-eligible based on the defined criteria.
- **Performance ratings:** **71 employees** did not have performance ratings, creating a potential gap in performance-based compensation assessment.
- **Bonus payout:** Total calculated bonus payouts were approximately **₦2.20 million**.
- **Overall compensation:** Total employee compensation analyzed was approximately **₦71.92 million**.
- **Department-level differences:** Salary and gender patterns varied across departments, highlighting areas where further investigation into compensation equity may be necessary.

## Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following actions are recommended:

- **Review salary structures:** Conduct a detailed review of salaries below the ₦90,000 threshold and assess whether adjustments are required to improve salary compliance.
- **Investigate departmental pay gaps:** Review the departments with the largest gender-related salary differences to understand the factors contributing to the observed gaps.
- **Strengthen performance evaluation:** Ensure employees receive timely and consistent performance ratings to support fair and transparent performance-based compensation.
- **Improve compensation transparency:** Establish clear and consistent criteria for salary progression, bonuses, and other forms of employee compensation.
- **Monitor gender representation:** Continue monitoring gender distribution across departments and regions to identify areas where representation may require attention.
- **Use regular HR analytics:** Implement periodic workforce and compensation analysis to monitor salary compliance, gender pay patterns, performance ratings, and bonus allocation over time.
